# AnonMarkets

## Privacy-First Prediction Markets on Solana

AnonMarkets is a decentralized prediction market protocol that combines the speed and low cost of Solana with strong privacy guarantees powered by Lit Protocol's MPC network.

---

## Key Features

### Zero Platform Fees
Trade without platform fees eating into your returns. The protocol generates revenue through staking vault deposits in liquid staking tokens.

### Private Bets, Public Odds
Market prices and total liquidity are visible to everyone, but individual positions remain private. Only you know your bets.

### Permissionless Markets
Anyone can create a market on any topic. No gatekeepers, no approval process.

### AI-Assisted Resolution
Markets are resolved by AI agents with a challenge period for disputes, ensuring fair and transparent outcomes.

---

## How It Works

1. **Connect** your Solana wallet
2. **Deposit** SOL into your private balance
3. **Bet** on market outcomes (YES or NO)
4. **Withdraw** your winnings or exit positions anytime

Your balance and positions are stored in a compressed merkle tree, making them invisible to observers while remaining fully verifiable.

## Technology Stack

- **Solana** - High-throughput, low-cost blockchain
- **SPL Account Compression** - Scalable merkle tree storage
- **Lit Protocol** - Decentralized MPC for private state management
- **Bonding Curve AMM** - Automated market maker for fair pricing

# Quick Start

Get started with AnonMarkets in under 5 minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack recommended)
- Some SOL for deposits and transaction fees

---

## Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit [anonmarkets.com](https://anonmarkets.com)
2. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top right
3. Select your wallet provider and approve the connection

---

## Step 2: Deposit SOL

Before placing bets, you need to deposit SOL into your private escrow balance.

1. Click **Deposit** in the header
2. Enter the amount of SOL you want to deposit
3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet

Your deposited funds are now held in a shared vault. Your individual balance is tracked privately in a merkle tree - only you can see how much you have.

---

## Step 3: Place a Bet

1. Browse available markets on the **Markets** page
2. Click on a market you're interested in
3. Choose **YES** or **NO** based on your prediction
4. Enter your bet amount
5. Review the shares you'll receive and confirm

Your bet is now placed! The market odds will update based on your trade.

---

## Step 4: Monitor & Exit

### Check Your Positions
- Go to the **Portfolio** page to see all your active bets
- Track current value and potential payout

### Exit Early (Optional)
You can sell your position before the market resolves:
1. Click on your position in Portfolio
2. Click **Sell**
3. Confirm the transaction

You'll receive SOL based on current market prices.

---

## Step 5: Claim Winnings

When a market resolves:

1. Go to **Portfolio**
2. Find the resolved market
3. If you won, click **Claim**
4. Your winnings are added to your available balance

---

## Step 6: Withdraw

To withdraw SOL back to your wallet:

1. Click **Withdraw** in the header
2. Enter the amount to withdraw
3. Confirm the transaction
4. SOL arrives in your wallet

---

## Tips for New Users

- **Start small** - Get familiar with the platform before making large bets
- **Understand the odds** - Lower probability events pay more but win less often
- **Check market details** - Read the resolution criteria before betting
- **Keep some SOL** - Leave some in your wallet for transaction fees
